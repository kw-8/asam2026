## Final record (Step 10)
- **Status:** dataset built and validated 2026-11-23 (`have-data`). Strategy-lab backtest run afterwards, outside this process (2026-11-23, `backtest.md`): the low-NOA portfolio never beats the equal-weighted universe on Sharpe (0.40 vs 0.48 design, 0.75 vs 0.74 holdout) and its design alpha is gone after 2009; adding `noa` to the defensive composite does not help. Not useful as a long-only sleeve.
- **Definition:** `noa` (primary) = (operating assets − operating liabilities) / total assets of the previous fiscal year, from the latest annual 10-K public by the month-end. Operating assets = `at` − `che`; operating liabilities = `at` − `dlc` − `dltt` − `mib` − `pstk` − `ceq`; so `noa` = (`ceq` + `pstk` + `mib` + `dltt` + `dlc` − `che`) / `at_prev`. Missing `che`, `dlc`, `dltt`, `mib`, `pstk` = 0; `at` > 0 and `ceq` required; previous fiscal year-end 10–14 months earlier, `at` > 0. Every firm gets a value; `financial` flag = CRSP SIC 6000–6999 that month. Signal on the primary (CCM-linked) share class; usable until 18 months after fiscal year-end; raw values. Side columns: `noa_mi` (robustness only; minority-interest fix from fiscal 2009), `noa_at_cur` (÷ this year's assets), `dnoa`, `asset_growth`; parts of NOA in the event file.
- **Timing:** 10-K filing date (`comp.co_filedate`), UTC clock → New York; before 4 p.m. → that trading day, otherwise next trading day; no 10-K date → fiscal year-end + 6 months (`assumed`, 9.1% of signal rows). `version = restated`.
- **Citation:** Hirshleifer, Hou, Teoh & Zhang (2004), "Do Investors Overvalue Firms with Bloated Balance Sheets?", *Journal of Accounting and Economics* 38(1), 297–331. See also Hou, Xue & Zhang (2020), "Replicating Anomalies," *RFS* 33(5), 2019–2133, doi:10.1093/rfs/hhy131 (`Noa`, `dNoa`); Chen & Zimmermann, Open Source Asset Pricing (`NOA`, `dNoa`).
- **Intuition:** NOA is the running total of reported operating income minus free cash flow. A high NOA means past earnings haven't turned into cash — through accruals, over-investment, and the equity or debt that paid for both. Investors with limited attention look at earnings, over-value these firms, and prices correct as profits fail to keep up. Slow: one to three years. Low NOA is not automatically healthy (e.g. Boeing: low NOA because customer advances and payables fund its assets).
- **Differences from the sources:**
  - *HHTZ (2004):* same formula. They rebalanced monthly with a 4-month lag on 1964–2002 data and kept financials; ours uses each firm's actual 10-K date and the panel's universe (1980–2025), with a `financial` flag.
  - *Hou-Xue-Zhang `Noa`:* same formula; they fix the value each June and exclude financials and negative-book-equity firms.
  - *Open Source Asset Pricing `NOA`:* leaves short-term debt (`dlc`) out of the numerator (and for some firms preferred stock) — kept as a known difference; ours follows the paper. Its `dNoa` matches our `dnoa` exactly (99.9% of same-fiscal-year rows). OSAP uses a fixed 6-month lag.
- **Known limitations:** (1) ASC 842 (fiscal 2019+): operating-lease liabilities are inside Compustat's debt, raising NOA for lease-heavy firms (retail & restaurants median 0.56 → 0.86 in the adoption year, then ≈ +0.1); not fixed — lease items are only in Compustat Snapshot, which this subscription can't read. (2) Compustat's restated values, not as first reported. (3) Extremes from SPACs/shells with ~$0 prior-year assets — rank or winsorize. (4) 4.1% of signal rows have negative equity. (5) 9.1% of signal rows use the assumed 6-month date (more in the early 1980s). (6) Paper published December 2004, so 2005–2025 is out of sample.
- **Rule for the backtest (fixed before any test):** `noa` is the result; `noa_mi` only answers "did the 2009 accounting change matter?".

## Step 4b — Definition ✅ (approved 2026-11-23: **A** as the signal, **B** as a side column; C dropped)

  - Channel columns from the same pull (approved in Step 1), all ÷ previous year's `at`: `dnoa` (change in NOA), `asset_growth` (`at` / previous `at` − 1); operating side `rect`, `invt`, `ppent`, `intan` (incl. goodwill, with `gdwl` separately), other operating assets (the rest of OA), operating liabilities OL (with `ap` separately); financing side equity (`ceq` + `pstk` + `mib`), debt (`dlc` + `dltt`), cash (`che`).

- **B. NOA ÷ this year's assets** (`noa_at_cur`). Same numerator, current `at` as denominator. Pure balance-sheet make-up: not inflated when assets jump (an acquisition that doubles `at` roughly doubles A, not B) and needs no previous year, so first-year firms get a value. Differs from the papers. Same fields as A → recommended as a free check column, not the main signal.
- **C. Quarterly NOA** (`comp.fundq`: `atq, cheq, dlcq, dlttq, mibq, pstkq, ceqq`, each public at its `rdq`), scaled by `atq` four quarters earlier. ### Two accounting breaks (found in the Step 7 checks; decide before Step 8)
1. **Noncontrolling interest, from fiscal 2009 (FAS 160).** Compustat `mib` now holds only *redeemable* noncontrolling interest; the *nonredeemable* part sits in equity but not in `ceq`, so the HHTZ formula counts it as an operating liability and NOA comes out lower. Gap `at − lt − ceq − pstk − mib` is 0 for every year before 2009, then > 1% of assets in 8–11% of firm-years (88% positive). Walmart FY2025 gap = 6,408 = its "nonredeemable noncontrolling interest" exactly. Fix with data already pulled: operating liabilities = `lt` − `dlc` − `dltt` (identical to HHTZ before 2009).
2. **Operating leases, from fiscal 2019 (ASC 842).** Compustat adds operating-lease right-of-use assets to `ppent` and operating-lease liabilities to `dlc`/`dltt` (Yueran Ma, "Note on Operating Lease Accounting Post 2019"; confirmed on Walmart above). Under the formula the lease liability counts as debt (financing), so NOA rises by roughly the lease liability for lease-heavy firms. Median change in (`dlc`+`dltt`)/`at` in fiscal 2019: +0.12 retail (SIC 53–59), +0.10 restaurants, +0.01 everyone else (≈ 0 in 2016–18). Fix needs Compustat's operating-lease liability items (`llc` current, `lllt` long-term, per Ma's note) — a small extra pull; their availability in `comp.funda` to be checked.
- Both breaks are also in HXZ's and OSAP's versions (same formula, same Compustat), so the Step 9 cross-check would match the unadjusted formula.
- **Options:**
  1. *(recommended)* Signal `noa` = the published HHTZ formula (comparable with HXZ/OSAP; Step 9 cross-check), plus a side column `noa_adj` that removes both breaks (NCI via `lt`; operating leases taken out of debt with `llc` + `lllt`). Needs a short Part 2 pull for the lease items. The backtest runs both, which shows whether the breaks matter after 2009/2019.
